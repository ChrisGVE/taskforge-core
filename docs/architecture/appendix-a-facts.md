# taskforge — Architecture, Appendix A: the fact corpus

This appendix belongs to `ARCHITECTURE.md`. It holds every measured fact the architecture cites, by identifier: `TW1` …
`TW26`, `TW31` and `TW32` for Taskwarrior; `X6` … `X10`, `X12`, `X13` and `X16` for cargo, rmcp, Rust, C, Lua and
JavaScriptCore. Identifiers of retired facts are not reused. Each fact has its command (abridged where the probe is
code), its result, the design rule it drives and the date and load at which it was taken. **Where each probe lives**
(the one statement; section 5.2 points here): the Taskwarrior facts in `taskforge-core/tests/tw_facts/`, except
TW21–TW24, which stay recorded measurements (their databases are built and polled through SQLite, which the build does
not carry) re-measured by `doctor --timings` and PN13; the Rust facts of X9 and the JavaScriptCore fact X10 in
`taskforge-core/tests/`; the C and Lua fact X16 with the comment encoder's tests; the cargo facts X7 and X12 as the CI
checks of 8.11 and 9.6. X13 has none, because it was read from rmcp's source and not executed (10.3); X8 is a simulation
of the merge that `merge_converges_with_rewriting_stores` replaces; X6 documents why taskforge runs no backup import of
its own.

**How every probe ran.** macOS, `task` 3.5.0 at `/usr/local/bin/task`, `cargo`/`rustc` 1.99.1, clang from LLVM, Lua
5.5.1, JavaScriptCore at its fixed system path, offline except the download of the rmcp crate into a throwaway directory
(X13). Every process ran under `env -i` with `PATH` and with `HOME`, `XDG_CONFIG_HOME`, `XDG_DATA_HOME`,
`XDG_CACHE_HOME`, `TASKRC` and `TASKDATA` (before 2026-11-07 also `XDG_STATE_HOME`) inside one throwaway directory,
printed and checked before the first probe; no real taskrc, database or configuration was read, no OmniFocus was
contacted and no `osascript` was run. Times are medians unless a range is given, with the 1-minute load average at the
time (the machine was shared; load ranged from 8 to 754). "Large" is a database of 21,401 rows (1,400 pending tasks and
20,001 completed or deleted ones), "small" the same 1,400 pending tasks alone; the databases of TW21–TW24 hold 10,
1,400, 5,000 and 10,000 pending tasks and no history, built by inserting rows into the `tasks` and `working_set` tables
of a database `task` had created. A fact marked "2026-11-03" or "2026-11-07" was measured on that date; "2026-11-05"
measured or re-measured then; the others on 2026-11-04.
